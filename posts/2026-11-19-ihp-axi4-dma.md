We upgraded [ihp-sg13g2-axi4-dma](https://github.com/NiranjankumarKatragadda/ihp-sg13g2-axi4-dma) from single-beat transfers to **16-beat AXI4 INCR bursts** (`AWLEN`/`ARLEN` up to 15) while keeping the RTL Verilog-2001/Icarus-compatible.

### Key Verification & Physical Design Results
- **Cocotb + `cocotbext-axi`:** Verified single-beat and multi-beat burst transfers (`TESTS=2 PASS=2` in 0.05s) across RTL and post-PnR gate-level netlists.
- **LibreLane on IHP SG13G2:** Closed synthesis, floorplanning, CTS, routing, and DRC/LVS signoff in GitHub Actions (run `44108818810`, 11m51s).
- **Takeaway:** Pairing `cocotbext-axi` burst scoreboards with automated RTL + gate-level CI catches burst-boundary and reset-timing bugs before tapeout.
